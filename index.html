About Upcodez

Upcodez is a client-side utility for inspecting publicly accessible HTML source through a CORS-compatible retrieval service.

Version 1.0 · Built with vanilla JS, Prism.js for highlighting.

Privacy Upcodez does not require an account and does not intentionally store inspected website source on its own server. All requests are made directly from your browser.

Source retrieval via backend API. Dynamically generated content may not appear.
