I Built RING FOR ME — A Different Way to Discover People Nearby ✦ Someone nearby. One Ring away. I’ve been working on something I’ve wanted to turn into a real product for a while: RING FOR ME — a proximity-based social discovery app built around one simple interaction: a Ring. 🌐 Live website: https://ringforme.zishuai.cloud 📱 Android APK: https://github.com/zishutron/Stardust-store/releases/download/v3.2.0/ringforme.apk --- The idea Most social platforms are built around feeds, followers, likes, or endless scrolling. I wanted to explore a different idea: What if discovering people started with who is actually around you? That became the foundation of RING FOR ME. The concept is intentionally simple: Discover → Ring → Connect You discover people nearby, check out their profile, and if someone catches your attention, you can send them a Ring. If they Ring back, it becomes a mutual Ring. No complicated introduction. Just one simple signal that can start an interaction. --- How RING FOR ME works 01 — Discover RING FOR ME is built around proximity-based discovery. The app shows profiles around you so you can discover who's nearby. 02 — Ring When you find someone you want to interact with, simply tap the Ring button. That's the core interaction of the product. 03 — Connect If the other person Rings back, you get a mutual connection. The idea is to keep the first step lightweight and let the interaction develop naturally. --- The Ring is the main idea I didn't want the product to be another interface full of complicated actions. The Ring is meant to be the simplest possible expression of interest: «“Hey, I noticed you.”» That single interaction became the visual and functional identity of the entire app. The UI, animations, notifications and even the landing page revolve around that idea. --- Building the actual product RING FOR ME isn't just a concept or a landing page. I built the working experience around several parts: - Nearby discovery - Profile cards - User profiles - Ring interaction - Incoming Ring experience - Mutual Rings - Ring history - Ringed section - Ghost Mode - Settings - Android app The goal was to make the experience feel consistent from the first screen to the moment a Ring is received. --- The visual direction The app uses a dark, minimal interface with warm gold accents. I wanted the design to feel: - Minimal - Premium - Slightly mysterious - Technology-focused - Human - Calm rather than noisy The radar / proximity / signal concept became a major part of the visual language. The landing page follows the same design system so the website feels like an extension of the product rather than a separate marketing page. --- The real app experience The landing page includes actual screens from the application rather than fake product mockups. The experience includes: Discover — See who's around you. Profile — Show a little about yourself. Ringed — Track your connections. History — See your previous Rings. Settings — Manage your profile and preferences. Ringing — Experience an incoming Ring. You can explore the full experience here: 🌐 https://ringforme.zishuai.cloud --- Privacy matters too Because proximity is a core part of the experience, privacy shouldn't be an afterthought. RING FOR ME includes Ghost Mode, a privacy-oriented visibility control that lets users control whether they're visible to others. You can read the full policies here: 🔐 Privacy Policy: https://zishutron.github.io/ringforme/privacy 📄 Terms of Service: https://zishutron.github.io/ringforme/terms --- What I'm exploring RING FOR ME is still early. I'm not trying to claim that I've solved social discovery. I'm exploring whether a simpler interaction model can make discovering people nearby more interesting. Instead of: scroll → scroll → scroll the idea is: discover → Ring → see what happens That's the experiment. --- Try it yourself The Android version is available as a direct APK. 📱 Download RING FOR ME: https://github.com/zishutron/Stardust-store/releases/download/v3.2.0/ringforme.apk Or explore the landing page: 🌐 https://ringforme.zishuai.cloud I'd genuinely love to hear what you think. What would you change? What feature would you want next? And most importantly — would you Ring someone nearby? ✦ --- What's next? The current version is only the beginning. My focus now is on: - Getting real users - Understanding how people use the Ring interaction - Improving the overall experience - Learning from feedback - Exploring sustainable growth and monetization - Building the next version around actual usage I'd rather let real users shape the product than build everything based on assumptions. --- Final thought The idea behind RING FOR ME is intentionally small: Someone nearby. One Ring away. But sometimes a small interaction can be the beginning of something bigger. Thanks for reading — and if you try it, I'd love to hear your thoughts. ✦ --- Disclosure: This article was created with AI assistance and reviewed/edited by the author.
